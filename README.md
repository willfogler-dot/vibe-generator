# Vibe Generator

TV for music: a warm, half-real half-fantasy valley you can leave running while music plays. Hands off, it plays like a film; take the controls whenever you want. Be a bird, a spirit, or a little adventurer on foot strolling the hiking trail.

**What plays along with the music**
- **Bloom meadows:** every chord change sends a wave through the meadows. Flowers burst open and take the chord's colour (minor chords lean cooler), then relax back to wildflower colours.
- **Chime circles:** rings of twelve standing stones; each note lights the stone for its pitch.
- **Villages:** hobbit folk dance round the campfire on the beat (they sit by the fire when there's no beat), windmill sails turn a quarter turn per bar, windows breathe with the bass, and at dusk and night the village sends up sky lanterns on chord changes and big moments.
- **The wider world:** the elves at the great tree raise their lights on chord changes, the stone troll drums on big moments, rabbits hop on the beat, a witch crosses the moon at night trailing sparks on the notes, mushroom fairy rings pulse as the bass rolls through, fish leap and splash down on notes, and the note orb hops stone to stone.

With an audio file the whole song is scanned for notes before it plays, so things land exactly on time; jazz covers know their notes; live tab/mic audio follows the predicted beat. Toggle each toy in Settings.

**Controls:** WASD or arrow keys steer, Shift to soar or run, drag to look around, scroll to zoom. Let go for a few seconds and the autopilot eases back. Click things: villagers jump, the campfire flares, houses release lanterns, the troll waves, standing stones chime, water ripples, trees release birds, windmills spin. With a beat locked, touches land on the next beat.

## Try it
1. Open the site (Chrome or Edge recommended) and wait for "Building the world…" to finish.
2. Click **Music source** and pick one:
   - **Play an audio file**: most reliable; any MP3/M4A/WAV/FLAC.
   - **Share a tab or system audio**: Chrome/Edge. Use the Spotify web player tab with "Share tab audio", or on Windows "Entire screen" + "Share system audio".
   - **Use the microphone**: works anywhere, lowest quality.
3. **Settings** lets you pin the time of day, choose what you are (bird, spirit, on foot, or just the view), toggle cinema bars, and switch music toys on or off.

There is no direct Spotify connection. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
