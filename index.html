Let the visuals hear the music

  1. Click Share now. Your browser asks what to share.
  2. Pick the Entire screen tab and select your screen.
  3. Turn on Also share system audio at the bottom, then click Share.
Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. The page only measures the sound to drive the visuals. Stop anytime from this button or the browser's sharing bar.
